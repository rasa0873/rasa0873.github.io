Personal Data: VZLA Cambio does not require user registration, accounts, or login credentials. We do not collect, request, store, or process any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, physical address, phone number, or government identifiers.

  • Device model and operating system version.

  • Performance metrics, app response times, and error/crash reports.

The app relies on trusted third-party infrastructure and open market feeds to deliver real-time exchange rates and cryptocurrency indicators. These third-party service providers may process anonymous diagnostic data in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

Since VZLA Cambio does not collect user accounts or personal profiles, we do not store personal databases on external servers. Local preferences (such as selected theme or favorite currencies) are saved locally on your device's internal storage and can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the application or clearing app data in your device settings.

Our application does not address or target anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.
